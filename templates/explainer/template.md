# Explainer

The default way oldguy tells a flow: one off-screen narrator, polite, cheery and efficient, and a scene that builds up
on screen as each sentence is spoken.

- **Who says what:** every sentence is spoken by the narrator. Sentences carry no `speaker`.
- **One exchange:** there is none; the narrator explains one idea per chapter in short, plain sentences.
- **Tone:** the house voice in `references/narrate.md`.
- **The slot:** the whole frame. Use the ready pieces or a designed scene, as `references/visuals.md` says.

**Gate:** no sentence has a `speaker`, and each chapter aims for 20 to 40 seconds.
